# Validation report: OpenLiDARViewer v0.6.7

This report states what v0.6.7 validates and what it does not. It is the human-readable companion to the machine-readable claim register (`docs/validation/claim-register.yaml`).

v0.6.7 is a licensing and engineering release that also promotes five evidence claims. The license moves to AGPL-3.0-only, the read paths are hardened against malformed and oversized input, and two ingest paths are added (out-of-core streaming for heavy local files, and 3D Tiles implicit tiling); none of that engineering promotes a product or carries an evidence grade. Separately, five products reach `E4_CROSS_IMPLEMENTATION_VALIDATED` this cycle, taking the register from twelve to seventeen products at E4. Each promotion is a cross-implementation check on frozen synthetic or analytic fixtures; none is field accuracy against a surveyed reference, and no registered product reaches E5. The grades carried forward from v0.6.6 are not restated here beyond the summary below; the authoritative statement of each is `VALIDATION_REPORT_v0.6.6.md`.

## Five products reach E4 this cycle

The register now holds seventeen products at `E4_CROSS_IMPLEMENTATION_VALIDATED`. Twelve are the products validated in v0.6.6, whose grades and boundaries are stated in `VALIDATION_REPORT_v0.6.6.md` and hold without change: `SLOPE-RASTER`, `ASPECT-RASTER`, `HILLSHADE`, `CONTOURS` and `MEAS-AREA` against GDAL; `DSM`, `DTM` and `CHM` against PDAL 2.10.2 `writers.gdal`; the terrain descriptors `TPI` (against gdaldem 3.13.1) and `VRM` (against SAGA 7.8.2), each also against the closed form on a controlled analytic fixture; `MEAS-PROFILE` against a reference assembled from OGR/SpatiaLite 5.1.0 and R 4.4.1; and `E57-INGEST` against PDAL `readers.e57`. The five added this cycle are:

- `CRS-UTM-PROJECTION` reaches E4 as a new claim. A WGS-84 latitude and longitude projected to a WGS-84 UTM zone, hemisphere, easting and northing was checked against GeographicLib 2.7 and PROJ 9.8.1 over 36 frozen geodetic fixtures (study `CRS-UTM-PROJ-GEOGRAPHICLIB-THIRTYSIX-FIXTURES`, protocol `PROTO-CRS-UTM-PROJ-GEOGRAPHICLIB`). Zone and hemisphere matched on all 36, including the widened Norway zone 32V and the Svalbard zones 31X, 33X, 35X and 37X; easting and northing agreed with both implementations to under 1.5 mm, the largest being 4.531e-4 m in easting and 9.521e-4 m in northing. PROJ and GeographicLib agree with each other to 2.0e-9 m, six orders below the gate, so the residual is attributable to the candidate. Scope: the datum is WGS 84 on both sides, so no datum transformation, no PROJ transformation grid, no vertical reference and no ECEF conversion is exercised; latitudes outside 80S to 84N are refused rather than extrapolated; and agreement with two implementations is not accuracy against a surveyed monument. The protocol freeze is adopted-with-result, not preregistered.
- `CHANGE-RASTER` reaches E4 from E2. Per-cell change was checked against GRASS 8.5.0 `r.mapcalc` over eight synthetic epoch pairs under protocol `PROTO-CHANGE-GRASS-MAPCALC` (study `CHANGE-RASTER-GRASS-MAPCALC-EIGHT-PAIRS`). Both sides select the same 3007 comparable cells, including a pair with holes in one epoch, and the largest relative difference is 7e-6 under a 1e-5 gate derived from Float32 storage. Scope: only one-metre metre-CRS square-cell grids were compared; agreement on synthetic epoch pairs is not accuracy against surveyed field change; per-cell LoD95 is pending and co-registration error is absent by construction.
- `CHANGE-VOLUME` reaches E4 from E2. Integrated cut and fill volume was checked against GRASS 8.5.0 `r.univar` over the same eight synthetic epoch pairs, and against the closed-form integral each pair was built from, agreeing with both to within 1e-6 (study `CHANGE-VOLUME-GRASS-MAPCALC-EIGHT-PAIRS`). The raster scope applies, and change-volume uncertainty stays exploratory.
- `HOLDOUT-RMSE` reaches E4 from E3. The internal holdout accuracy statistics were recomputed in base R 4.6.1 over six frozen residual vectors (study `ACCURACY-RMSE-R-RESIDUALS`): bias, RMSE and max absolute residual agree to about 4.4e-16. The R leg establishes the computation, not accuracy: it confirms RMSE is the raw second moment and not a standard deviation. These are internal holdout diagnostics, so the held-out points share the source of the interpolated ones; this is not independent checkpoint accuracy and not ASPRS compliance, and the claim still requires E5. Median, NMAD and P95 are excluded from the R comparison because R interpolates quantiles at type 7 while the code takes the nearest rank.
- `NVA-VVA` reaches E4 from E3. The 95 percent figure (1.96 times the pooled RMSE) was recomputed in base R 4.6.1 over the same six residual vectors and agrees to about 8.9e-16 (study `ACCURACY-NVA95-R-RESIDUALS`). The R leg confirms the multiplier is applied correctly, not that the figure is field accuracy: these are internal holdout diagnostics, not ASPRS NVA/VVA compliance, and the claim still requires E5.

`GROUND-FILTER` stays partial, every other terrain product tops out at E3, and no registered product reaches E5.

## What the engineering work is, and how it is covered

The memory-safety hardening and the two new ingest paths change how the viewer behaves on a hostile, truncated or oversized file, and how it loads a large one. They do not change what it computes on a valid file, so they carry no evidence grade of their own. They are covered by the test suite rather than by a scientific claim:

- The shared decoded-byte budget and the fail-closed refusals are exercised by unit tests over malformed and over-ceiling inputs, which assert the read is refused before allocation rather than after.
- The out-of-core store lifecycle is exercised by tests that drive each cancel and attach path and assert the store is either committed or deleted, including the short-write and abandoned-store cases.
- The out-of-core streaming and 3D Tiles implicit-tiling paths are exercised by ingest tests over their respective fixtures.
- The corrected behaviours below are each pinned by a test: the profile PDF max-grade sign, the Inspector vertical-unit rows, the panel-rail observer disposal, the degenerate-profile coverage report, and the profile-workbench close restoring the Analyse panel.

None of these is a validated accuracy figure, because none of them produces a number to validate against a reference.

## The licensing change carries no evidence implication

The move to AGPL-3.0-only is a license change over this project's own source. It does not alter the license of any bundled dependency or of any test or validation dataset, and it does not touch the claim register, the reference slots or any study manifest. No evidence claim depends on it.

## What was tested for v0.6.7

Whole-suite evidence for this release comes from the release-mode gate run at the tagged commit, with an exit marker per blocking stage in the shipped `gate.log`. The authoritative record is the release asset `test-evidence-v0.6.7.json`; its SHA-256 is in `SHA256SUMS`, and `release-manifest-v0.6.7.json` binds the tag to the full 40-character commit and to every artifact hash, which `npm run release:verify` walks. Published totals are read from `docs/validation/test-evidence.json` rather than entered by hand, and `npm run lint:evidence` checks the documents against it. The reference-slot honesty tests (`tests/crossCheck.test.ts`, `tests/crossImplementationManifest.test.ts`) and the study verifier (`npm run validation:study:verify`) confirm the supplied slots, including the five added this cycle, and that the rest stay pending.

## What was NOT tested (and is not claimed)

Real-terrain DSM and DTM accuracy, ground-classification accuracy, void interpolation, mixed-unit gridding, and any field-grade or survey-grade figure. Nothing in the memory-safety wave or the new ingest paths is a claim about accuracy; each is a claim about refusal and lifecycle behaviour on a bad or large input, bounded by the tests named above. The external DTM checkpoint results reported below the grade line in earlier releases remain external agreement on found public data, not a preregistered field campaign, and do not move a grade here.
