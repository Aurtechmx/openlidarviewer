# Validation report: OpenLiDARViewer v0.6.9

## No product changed evidence level

The register holds 34 claims: 2 at E1, 6 at E2, 9 at E3 and 17 at E4. Nothing
reached E5. No claim changed level, and no claim was added or removed. This
release promotes nothing, so there is no new cross-implementation or field
result to report.

One measurement method changed. The interactive stockpile volume moved from the
point-sample estimator `olv.volume.stockpile` to the area-grid estimator
`olv.volume.stockpile-area-grid`, generation 2, which clips each cell to the
polygon and takes the median surface within the cell. VOL-STOCKPILE stays at E3.
The method is checked against synthetic known truth and its field accuracy is
unestablished, so the change removes a density sensitivity rather than earning a
level. A figure computed from a source that is not fully resident is reported as
PREVIEW, and renderer readiness does not stand in for source completeness.

The canonical stockpile record outside the lasso toast is not integrated. The
session record, the report and the measurement CSV still carry the point-sample
cut and fill volume record, and nothing here validates the area-grid figure as
that record.

## What the engineering work is, and how it is covered

The progressive local LAZ open, the persistent TerrainCore on OPFS, the bounded
worker fallback, the four numeric rewrites and the shell decomposition are
engineering. They are covered by the deterministic gate rather than by a study,
because none of them changes a terrain number.

The byte-identity gates are the load-bearing checks for the four rewrites. The
contiguous DTM aggregation, the indexed ICP target, the typed voxel accumulator
and the two square-opening implementations are each held to identical output
against the code they replace, so the recorded speedups are timing results and
not numerical ones. The measurements are in
`docs/validation/local-laz-open-baseline.json`,
`docs/validation/terrain-core-opfs-restore-baseline.json` and
`docs/validation/sync-fallback-budget-baseline.json`, each at the revision
recorded inside it rather than at the final commit.

The persistent core is keyed by the identity of the analysed input, not of the
file, and any mismatch recomputes. The benchmark record reports byte-identical
grids between a fresh compute and a restore, on Chromium against real OPFS at
revision c14cc2c1 plus the benchmark commit. It is a restore-versus-compute
measurement at that revision, not a final-commit figure.

The worker fallback budgets are measured in Node as a proxy for the main thread
on an Apple M3 Max. A workload past the budget refuses with a typed error
stating nothing was produced, so an over-budget case cannot silently produce a
figure on the main thread.

## What was NOT tested, and is not claimed

- No field accuracy result that promotes a product. No product is at E5, and the
  Rogue holdout has not been run.
- The area-grid stockpile volume is not validated against surveyed field volume.
  It is checked against synthetic known truth at E3, as the point-sample
  estimator was.
- Touch gestures were not exercised on WebKit or Firefox in CI. See the known
  limitations.
- The registration stack, the artifact passport, the evidence boundary inspector
  and the DTM surface digest have unit tests and no user path, so nothing here
  validates them as features.
- Three measurement figures have a documented basis problem that this release
  did not correct: the resident-versus-declared density basis, the boundary
  share, and the principal-component bounding box. Each is listed in
  `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS_v0.6.9.md` with what it discloses.
- Mutation testing runs on its own schedule and is not part of this gate. The
  record cites its last result and the commit it was measured at.
