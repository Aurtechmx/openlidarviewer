# Commercial licensing

OpenLiDARViewer v0.6.7 and later is distributed under `AGPL-3.0-only`. For most
users, including researchers and open-source projects, that is all you need.

Some organizations need rights the AGPL does not grant. A commercial license
from Aur Technologies can cover cases such as:

- embedding OpenLiDARViewer in a closed-source product;
- OEM redistribution under your own brand;
- proprietary modifications you do not wish to publish;
- hosting a modified version as a network service without the AGPL source offer;
- enterprise distribution under negotiated terms.

To ask about a commercial license, contact Aur Technologies at
alex@aurtech.mx. Please describe how you intend to use OpenLiDARViewer so the
right terms can be discussed.

## What a commercial license does and does not cover

Commercial rights are available only for the components for which Aur
Technologies holds sufficient licensing rights. OpenLiDARViewer's own code
qualifies. Third-party components keep their own licenses: a permissive
dependency such as an MIT or Apache-2.0 library can be used inside a proprietary
product under its own terms, but Aur Technologies does not relicense that
third-party code. Datasets and sample data are licensed by their publishers and
are not part of any commercial software license. See
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) and
[docs/project/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](docs/project/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
