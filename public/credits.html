Privacy

Files you open are processed on your device and are not uploaded. The app has no analytics and no telemetry. It uses your browser's local storage for session recovery (kept for up to 7 days), your preferences and local usage counts, and keeps a cache of its own files, plus the full offline copy if you choose to make one. None of it leaves your device. When you open a remote dataset or a sample, it is downloaded from the server that hosts it, and that server sees your IP address and the tiles you view. A location search in the public LiDAR catalogue is sent to Microsoft Planetary Computer, only when you run one, and opening a result sends a request to its signing service carrying that tile's address. See PRIVACY.md.

Validation datasets

Statistics and small samples derived from these datasets are committed to the repository as validation evidence. They are not shipped inside the app.

  • Jemez River Basin Snow-off Lidar Survey (2010), by the National Center for Airborne Laser Mapping (NCALM), distributed by OpenTopography. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Modified: decode and ground-filter agreement statistics were computed from tile ot_356000_3972000_1.laz; the point data is not redistributed. Register id OLV-DS-090.
  • 3DPC of a gypsum slope in Finestrat, Alicante (Spain) (2016 epoch), by A. Abellan and A. Riquelme, published on Zenodo. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Modified: eighteen points and summary statistics were extracted as a test reference; the scan is not redistributed. Register id OLV-DS-040.
  • LiDAR Scanners Comparison - Point Clouds (2025), by M. Hrdina, published on Zenodo. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Modified: 2,000 records of the LA03 scan were copied into a small test file, and the published reference-point coordinates were rounded into a CSV; the scans and workbook are not redistributed. Register ids OLV-DS-054 and OLV-DS-056.
  • TauDEM for Bear Creek (2023), by Cybercastor, published on Zenodo. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Modified: two windows of the DEM and the TauDEM filled DEM were cut and re-encoded as test references; the archive is not redistributed. Register id OLV-DS-117.
  • Flow direction and accumulation maps, Reunion island, with the Riviere des Pluies catchment (2026), by N. Poncet et al., published on Zenodo. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Modified: one window of the elevation, direction and accumulation rasters and the rasterised catchment were re-encoded as a test reference; the source files are not redistributed. The elevations derive from the BD ALTI 25 m of the Institut national de l'information géographique et forestière (IGN), published by IGN under Licence Ouverte 2.0 (Etalab); the Zenodo depositor does not state the upstream licence. Register id OLV-DS-118.
  • RichDEM test and example grids, by Richard Barnes, from r-barnes/richdem at commit 9a1c97bb. Licensed GPL-3.0. Six files are kept unmodified as test references; no RichDEM code is included. Register ids OLV-DS-122 to OLV-DS-127.
  • 2019 USFS Lidar: Rogue River-Siskiyou National Forest, tile 10TDM3449, by the USDA Forest Service and the U.S. Geological Survey 3D Elevation Program, catalogued at NOAA InPort item 64356. Public domain; credited as a courtesy. Register id OLV-DS-093.
