# Intake corpus: evaluation protocol

This protocol governs phase C of the open-any-point-cloud intake: the generic
recovery of headerless fixed-record binary point streams and delimited text
point lists. Recovery output is a preview only. It is shown to users only after
the recovery code has passed the pre-registered criteria in `criteria.json` on
the held-out split described here.

## Pre-registration

`criteria.json` and this file were committed on their own, before the corpus
generator and before any recovery code existed. The thresholds may not be
changed after any held-out result has been seen. A change after that point
voids the evaluation: a fresh held-out split with new seeds has to be generated
and registered before the next measurement.

## Corpus

The corpus is generated by `generate.mjs` from fixed integer seeds. It has two
labels and two splits.

Positive samples are headerless point streams. Their points come from small
deterministic subsamples of two register datasets, OLV-DS-090 (licence
CC-BY-4.0) and OLV-DS-093 (public domain), and from synthetic scenes. They are
written in several record layouts: float32 and float64 XYZ, scaled int32 XYZ,
XYZ with a 16-bit intensity, XYZ with 8-bit RGB, record strides from 12 to 48
bytes, little and big byte order, and with or without leading header bytes.
A few positives are delimited text point lists.

Negative samples are binaries and text that are not point clouds: random bytes,
deflate-compressed data, PCM-like audio, raw image pixels, float32 arrays that
are not geometry (one-dimensional signals, sorted sequences, mesh vertex
buffers with index buffers interleaved), text logs, and CSV tables with no
spatial columns.

No other data source is used. Every real point comes from a register dataset
whose licence permits redistribution.

## Splits

The tuning split may be used freely while developing recovery. The held-out
split uses different seeds, different real points (the tuning split takes even
positions of each seed file, the held-out split odd positions) and record
layouts that do not appear in the tuning split. The layout key of every
held-out positive is absent from the tuning split, and no sample id or content
hash appears in both splits. `tests/intakeCorpusManifest.test.ts` checks this.

## Procedure

1. Regenerate the corpus with `node validation/intake-corpus/generate.mjs --out <dir>`
   and confirm every content hash matches `manifest.json`.
2. Run the recovery on every held-out sample with no access to the manifest.
3. Score each sample with the definitions in `criteria.json`.
4. Publish the full per-sample outcome table, including every false positive
   and every failed coordinate check.
