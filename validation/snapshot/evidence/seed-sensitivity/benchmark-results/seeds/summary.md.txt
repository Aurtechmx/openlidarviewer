# Seed sensitivity

How much of a reported figure is the pipeline and how much is the particular draw. The reproducibility suite fixes one seed and shows the pipeline repeats there; that is determinism, not stability. This sweep runs the same analysis over independently seeded fixtures from the same generator and the same distribution.

## Configuration

- seeds: 32, from 900017 to 1145506
- points per fixture: 60000
- cell size: 2.00 m
- quantile convention: type-7
- relative standard error of a CV at n = 32: 0.127 (1/sqrt(2(n-1))). A CV printed below is good to about ±13 % of itself at one standard error, and roughly ±25 % across a 95 % interval. It does not separate two CVs that differ by less than that.
- min and max are the two most extreme of 32 draws. They bound nothing about a rarer seed.

## What this sweep does not cover

- timing, memory and throughput — host-dominated, owned by the scaling suite
- browser-only quantities: GPU upload, frame rate, time to interaction
- real scan data — one synthetic generator, one surface model
- sensitivity to any parameter other than the seed
- the tail behaviour of rare seeds — 32 draws bound it not at all

The sweep is 32 draws from one synthetic generator on one machine at one commit. It is a statement about that generator, and it generalises to no field dataset.
