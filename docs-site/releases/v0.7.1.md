# v0.7.1

The walkthrough of every workflow in the packaged build was done on the public
Virginia Tech StREAM Lab Spring 2026 drone lidar scans (OpenTopography,
https://doi.org/10.5069/G9NZ85W7). The v0.7.0 release and its
records are unchanged: [v0.7.0](./v0.7.0.md).

Since v0.7.0, volumes and terrain outputs state only the evidence that covers them. A withheld grid volume withholds its cross-check too, the full-coverage lasso stockpile reads "exploratory volume preview", and box volume has its own claim, MEAS-BOX. The live DTM is E4 on measured cells only for cell sizes from 0.1 to 10 m with the grid origin at the data minimum, and a terrain product that matches no study is reported as not covered.

Terrain Flow Pulse moves to E4 after a comparison with TauDEM and GRASS GIS on public DEMs, and its depression inventory moves to E3. The register holds 41 claims, 18 of them at E4 and none at E5.

Closing or replacing a scan no longer leaves undo entries, clip boxes or panel state from the previous scene. Startup code is smaller, and large scans, E57 decoding and streaming are faster. Measuring, saving and error messages are clearer.

Five loader fixes come with a pinned corpus of upstream format test files. The scan report adds a Storage history row, a preview at E2. Named local coordinate systems keep their name, and change detection refuses two local frames with different names.

Two export fields are renamed: stockpile `grid_authority=measured` is now `exploratory_full_coverage`, and contour GeoJSON `meanConfidence` is now `presentationSupport`.

Figures on a sampled load say they come from a sample. Volumes show five significant figures. The profile's first chart draws heights at true scale, and its automatic corridor stops at a 5 m half-width on a scan with a known metre scale. A layer can be made active from the Data tab, and a Created layers list shows or hides the overlays. Profile exports name the scan the profile was measured on. A glTF or GLB is checked against its own buffers before any point is read.

Flow Pulse exports a drainage sheet PDF with a cover page, a site map with labelled catchments, catchment pages and a depression table, every page marked PREVIEW. The profile PDF prints at standard horizontal and vertical scales with a station band at round chainages. Flow Pulse and Terrain Access packages write a `.prj` beside their rasters when the scan has an EPSG code but no WKT. Make available offline checks every file against the SHA-256 in the build manifest before storing it, and two deployments on one origin keep separate offline copies.

Nothing here is a survey-grade result. The validation report lists retrospective checkpoint comparisons at Marsh Island and Coconino, which were not preregistered, and no prospective field study is bundled.
