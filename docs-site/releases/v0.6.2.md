# v0.6.2

A validation-and-correction release. Twelve validation suites were added, and
eighteen defects were fixed: twelve exposed by one of the new suites, six by
code review, and all eighteen missed by the test suite as it stood at v0.6.1,
which exits 0 on the defective code. Four of them reached published v0.6.1
output and are corrected in `docs/release/ERRATUM_v0.6.2.md`. Aspect and
hillshade join slope at E4 against GDAL 3.13.1 on the shared analytic DEM, and
cross-platform reproducibility is established on darwin-arm64 and linux-x64
from one seeded fixture at zero tolerance. Nothing here is field-validated, and
four targeted mutations still survive the full gate.

Full notes: [RELEASE_NOTES_v0.6.2.md](https://github.com/Aurtechmx/openlidarviewer/blob/main/RELEASE_NOTES_v0.6.2.md)
