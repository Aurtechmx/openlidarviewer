# v0.6.5

A Process Studio, feature-extraction, and terrain-evidence release. A Process
Studio panel in the left rail reports what a loaded scan can safely produce, each
product reading `ready`, `review`, or `blocked` with a plain reason, and it fails
closed on an unconfirmed unit or a missing vertical reference. First
feature-extraction and registration cores land: building-footprint candidates as
RFC 7946 GeoJSON, a conductor primitive, and a Kabsch/Horn plus trimmed-ICP
registration core with a non-destructive Float64 transform store.

Multi-layer mounting is enabled. Two georeferenced layers that declare the same
projected CRS place into one shared frame at their real separation,
non-destructively, with the Float64 per-layer-frame migration keeping every
boundary on the world coordinate in the frame it names.

Contours reach cross-implementation evidence (E4) against `gdal_contour`, and the
terrain evidence widens from synthetic fixtures onto real airborne LiDAR with two
absolute-accuracy checkpoint results. Remote streaming is hardened: signed URLs
scrubbed from error messages, bounded range reads, pinned object identity, and a
stable streaming shell id.
