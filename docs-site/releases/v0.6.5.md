# v0.6.5

A Process Studio, feature-and-registration cores, terrain-evidence and
streaming release. A Process Studio panel in the left rail reads the loaded
scan and reports what it can safely produce — DTM, DSM, contours, building
footprints, cross-epoch change, volume — as `ready`, `review`, or `blocked`
with a plain reason. It reads the same capability model the exporters read, so
the panel and a real export cannot disagree, and it fails closed: an
unconfirmed linear unit blocks the georeferenced products, a missing or
differing vertical reference blocks cross-epoch height math, and resident-only
streaming coverage cannot back a full-dataset product.

The first feature-extraction and registration cores land. Feature extraction
turns classified building points into footprint candidates and exports them as
RFC 7946 GeoJSON marked as derived candidates, not surveyed outlines.
Registration lands the rigid-alignment maths — a Kabsch/Horn solve, a trimmed
general ICP, tie-point alignment, and a non-destructive Float64 transform store
— with the two-scan mount held disabled until the per-layer frame work
completes. Both are pure, tested cores; their interactive surfaces are staged
for a later cycle.

The terrain evidence moves off synthetic fixtures onto public, cited field
data. OLV's DTM agrees with surveyed USGS checkpoints at 2.8 cm RMSE on the
Marsh Island UAS survey (101 of 104 RTK shots) and 3.9 cm on a single forested
Coconino checkpoint; slope's cross-check now also runs on real steep terrain
(agreement to 0.013° against gdaldem over ~20k cells); and the SMRF ground
filter agrees with PDAL on 0.99985 of 95,005 returns over a real boreal crop,
scoped to low-relief terrain. Contours move to
`E4_CROSS_IMPLEMENTATION_VALIDATED`, agreeing with `gdal_contour` to a maximum
separation of 2.9×10⁻⁵ m on a frozen analytic surface; survey-grade contours
remain a prohibited claim.

Remote streaming is hardened: signed EPT URLs are scrubbed from error messages,
every range read is bounded in bytes and time, the remote object's identity is
pinned across a load, and a streaming scan now carries a stable shell id so the
export and terrain guards catch a streaming-to-streaming swap. Multi-layer
mounting stays disabled and guarded while the per-layer frame fixes land.
