# v0.7.0

v0.7.0 reads ASPRS classes and classification flags from one place, adds two terrain tools, and tightens how units, datums and sampled data are stated. No existing claim changed evidence level: the register holds 37 claims, 17 of them at E4 and none at E5.

ASPRS class semantics now come from one module instead of a table in each of eight places, and names depend on the point data record format the file declares. Class 12 is Overlap Points under the legacy formats and reserved from format 6, where overlap is carried by a flag. Classes 19 to 22 are named rather than reported as user-defined.

Classification flags survive a load. Synthetic, Key-Point, Withheld and Overlap are read from both record layouts, carried on the point model and written back by both LAS writers. Terrain analysis, the volume tools, profiles, density, epoch comparison and feature extraction leave Withheld points out and record how many they excluded.

Terrain Flow Pulse routes flow over a terrain surface and reports depressions. Terrain Access screens a surface for traversability and finds a least-cost route. Both say when the terrain run behind them is a preview or blocked, and neither is a safety or hydrology result.

A layer the loader reduced to a display sample can be exported at full resolution or reloaded at a higher density, and anything written from a sample says so. Measurements and exports state the horizontal and vertical units they use, refuse a geographic CRS in degrees, and refuse datum shifts the application cannot apply.

Nothing here is a survey-grade result, and held-out reference validation has not been done. The limits are in the known limitations and the release notes under `docs/releases/`.
