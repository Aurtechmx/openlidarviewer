# v0.6.6

A focused evidence release. The DSM and DTM surface grids reach independent
cross-implementation evidence (E4). Each grid is recomputed from its point cloud
and compared against a committed PDAL 2.10.2 `writers.gdal` reference on three
seeded synthetic clouds, agreeing over 7,500 cells to a maximum difference under
4×10⁻⁶ m, within a 0.05 m tolerance registered before the references were
generated. Eight products are now at E4, the canopy height model (CHM) alongside them, as the clamped max minus min difference of the two PDAL grids.

The check covers the cell gridding: the reference radius is below half a cell, so
it does not exercise ground classification (`GROUND-FILTER` stays partial) or
real-terrain void interpolation, and the DTM's required bar for field validation
stays at E5. The terrain and measurement algorithms are inherited from v0.6.5
unchanged.
